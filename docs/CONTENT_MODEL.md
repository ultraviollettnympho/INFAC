# CONTENT MODEL — INFAC V0.2

Typed entities live in `src/data/`. No CMS yet. No invented local addresses.

## Entities

- **Project** — made work. `/work/:id`
- **Artist** — person or collective credit. `/artists/:id`
- **Place** — site. Addresses withheld when listing them would burn the place.
- **Event** — gathering, including lost/unconfirmed.
- **Resource** — provisions record with items (name, what, phone, url, hours, how, verify).
- **Theory** — dispatch | position | question | text.
- **Residue** — fragment with catalog id (R-001…).
- **Issue** — publication object with articles.
- **Article** — belongs to an issue.
- **Transmission** — dated field note; Signal surfaces the current one.

## Status

`active | dormant | unfinished | archived | contested | transformed | lost`

## Process tags

`nigredo | albedo | citrinitas | rubedo` are stored as a possible lifecycle hint. They are **not** displayed as doctrine.

## Seed policy

Prototype titles (Still Buried, The Long Table, Signal Bleed, the theory names, residue fragments) are kept as V0.1 field records.

Provisions items are real national services, dated 2026-09, with a verify line.

Threshold Edition is this website, credited as an INFAC work — not fictional history.

Hidden residue `R-093` and `R-418` exist for terminal discovery; they are not explained in navigation.
