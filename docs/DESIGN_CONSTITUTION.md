# DESIGN CONSTITUTION — INFAC V0.2

INFAC does not hide information. INFAC layers information.

Mystery is optional. Function is not.

## Seven principles

1. **Threshold** — entry is intentional, skippable, keyboard-operable, not repeated on every navigation.
2. **Chamber** — navigation is spatial in language; structural in URLs.
3. **Correspondence** — relationships are navigable.
4. **Revelation** — content can emerge; essential content is never locked.
5. **Residue** — unfinished material is legitimate.
6. **Seal** — objects have persistent symbolic identity via the registry.
7. **Clear channel** — Provisions remains immediately usable.

## Three registers

**Signal** — warm black, bone, phosphor. Atmosphere allowed. Editorial surface.

**Corpus** — paper, ink, blood, sick green. Print objects. Blackletter is not used as body (and currently not used at all).

**Provisions** — high contrast, sans, obvious labels, no buried info, no autoplay, no sigil-decoding to find a bed.

## Anti-rules

No generic pentagrams, Baphomet, fake Latin, occult starter-pack palettes as identity.
No WebGL cathedral. No autoplay sound.
No initiation required for ordinary visitors.
Do not mystify functionality.
