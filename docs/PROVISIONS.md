# PROVISIONS

Provisions is INFAC's clear channel.

It exists for information that may matter more than the interface around it: food, housing, harm reduction, care, crisis support, transportation, mutual aid, and directories.

## Rules

- Essential information is visible without symbolic decoding.
- Phone numbers are directly callable on supported devices.
- External resources are clearly identified as external.
- Verification dates are recorded whenever practical.
- Do not publish invented local addresses.
- Do not expose sensitive or precarious locations without a reason and stewardship decision.
- Resource records should distinguish official information from community notes.
- Expired information should be marked rather than silently presented as current.
- Crisis resources should never be turned into engagement mechanics.

## Future stewardship

A mature Provisions system should support:

```text
resource
  ├── source
  ├── verification date
  ├── verification method
  ├── region
  ├── category
  ├── visibility
  ├── community notes
  └── change history
```

The goal is not a giant directory. The goal is trustworthy practical information.
